## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a high-energy, clean, and modern athletic retail experience. Rooted in elite sports gear engineering, it communicates power, precision, and technical credibility without visual clutter. The aesthetic balances crisp light-mode clarity with deep heritage athletic accents and electric kinetic calls to action.

### Emotional Response & Personality
- **Disciplined & Powerful:** Structured grid lines and high-contrast typography evoke championship readiness and technical mastery.
- **Approachable & Modern:** Generous white space, soft floating pill contours, and tactile glass highlights keep the storefront approachable, premium, and fluid.
- **Dynamic Energy:** Punchy coral-orange accents inject urgency into conversions and promotional highlights without disrupting the serene editorial architecture.

### Visual Style
A hybrid of **Modern Athletic Minimalism** and **Tactile Product Staging**:
- Pristine light canvases (`#f8fafc` to `#ffffff`) serve as neutral stages that let sports equipment photography stand forward.
- High-contrast typography drives immediate reading hierarchy.
- Soft multi-stop ambient drop shadows lift product cards and floating navigation bars from the background plane, simulating premium physical packaging.

## Colors

The color palette is strictly balanced between deep sporting heritage and rapid conversion velocity:

- **Primary (`#043626` - Deep Racing Green):** Anchors brand presence, header icons, secondary structural buttons, product category indicators, and confidence badges. Reflects longevity, turf, and master craft.
- **Secondary (`#ff4d2d` - Vibrant Kinetic Orange):** Reserved exclusively for high-priority conversion points: flash sales, dynamic counter badges, promotional CTAs, and active hover underlines.
- **Tertiary (`#0f172a` - Deep Slate Navy):** The structural dark tone used for high-impact display headlines, primary search containers, and promotional banner slabs.
- **Neutral (`#f8fafc` - Crisp Canvas):** Soft, clean, low-glare surface color paired with pure white (`#ffffff`) card elevations and light borders (`#e2e8f0`).

### Role-Based Application
- **Surface Canvas:** `#f8fafc` for body background; `#ffffff` for elevated item containers.
- **Text Hierarchy:** Display headlines in `#0f172a`, secondary descriptive copy in `#64748b`, and inverted text on primary green/slate surfaces in `#ffffff`.
- **Status & Badges:** Green-tinted pills (`#ecfdf5` with `#043626` text) for stock availability and authentic factory certifications; Orange pills (`#fff1ee` with `#ff4d2d` text) for limited drops and discounts.

## Typography

Typography relies entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, exploiting its geometric proportions, contemporary humanist touch, and extreme clarity at both massive display weights and micro label scales.

- **Headlines & Banners:** Set in weights `700` and `800` with subtle negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) to inject athletic confidence, density, and punch.
- **Running Copy:** Set in weight `400` with relaxed line-height multipliers (1.5x - 1.6x) to preserve effortless scan-ability over light gray surfaces.
- **Labels & Micro-Tags:** Set in uppercase weight `800` (`label-caps`) for badges like "HIT", "NEW", or "3D SPATIAL NAVIGATOR", ensuring fast recognition at small viewport scales.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a responsive 12-column grid system paired with consistent vertical rhythm bands:

- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column grid, max content boundary `1440px`, with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` page margins. Section blocks alternate between `space-xl` and `space-2xl` padding to isolate categories cleanly.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout, `1rem` gutters, and `1.5rem` margins. Complex carousels and promotional cards collapse from 4-column tiers to 2-column or horizontal scroll strips.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `0.75rem` gutters, and `1rem` outer canvas padding. Category selector pills reflow into an edge-faded horizontally scrollable bar.

Grid spacing emphasizes generous breathing room around visual products, ensuring high-value equipment is viewed as an individual hero element rather than congested inventory.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages light-mode ambient shadows with microscopic structural borders to establish depth without heaviness.

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Background surfaces (`#f8fafc`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Elevation):** White card elements (`#ffffff`) framed by a subtle 1px border (`#e2e8f0`) and elevated by an ultra-soft ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Badges & Navigation):** Pill navigators, category filters, and floating verification chips use high-blur diffusion: `0 10px 30px -4px rgba(4, 54, 38, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Popovers & Modals):** Product quick views and drawer menus: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.
- **Product Staging Pedestal:** Product visuals feature an inner radial gradient vignette (`#ffffff` to `#f1f5f9`) giving products an authentic 3D studio bounce light.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates high-roundedness level `3` (Pill-shaped) geometry to convey speed, modern digital dexterity, and ergonomic equipment form factors.

- **Full Pills (`rounded-full` / `9999px`):** Used universally for search bars, action buttons, category filter tabs, badge indicators, and newsletter inputs.
- **Large Cards (`rounded-lg` / `2rem`):** Major surface containers, promotional dark banners, and hero product stages adopt a sweeping `2rem` corner radius, creating an inviting, tactile enclosure.
- **Micro UI (`rounded-md` / `0.75rem`):** Internal image preview thumbnails and secondary inset badges scale down proportionally to prevent visually distorted outer boundaries.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Full pill (`9999px`), solid secondary orange (`#ff4d2d`), white bold text (`label-lg`), with subtle kinetic elevation on hover (`translate-y-[-1px]` and soft orange shadow `rgba(255, 77, 45, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary CTA:** Full pill, deep racing green (`#043626`), white text, used for structural actions such as "Subscribe" or "Explore Disciplines".
- **Tertiary / Utility:** Pure white background pill, 1px border (`#e2e8f0`), text in dark slate (`#0f172a`), used for navigation controls (prev/next circular buttons) and wishlist triggers.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges ("HIT", "NEW"):** Compact pill chips positioned top-left of cards. "NEW" uses soft green tint (`#ecfdf5`) with primary green text; "HIT" uses cool gray tint (`#f1f5f9`) with charcoal text (`#334155`).
- **Category Filter Pills:** Floating pill container with active item indicated by subtle active text and an athletic coral underscore or soft active background fill.

### Cards
- **Standard Product Card:** White (`#ffffff`) background, `1.5rem` border-radius, hairline border (`#f1f5f9`), containing a dedicated light-gray staging thumbnail (`#f8fafc` rounded `1rem`), followed by product title in bold, specification summary in muted slate, bottom-aligned price in bold display typography, and a circular outline favorite button.
- **Hero Promo Banner:** Dark charcoal/slate slate (`#0f172a`) curved block (`2rem`), featuring horizontal flex arrangement with visual icon left, stacked urgent promotional copy center, and secondary kinetic orange CTA button right.

### Input Fields
- **Search Bar:** High-profile pill (`9999px`) on pure white background, framed by hairline border (`#e2e8f0`), deep slate placeholder text (`#94a3b8`), ending in an integrated circular primary green icon button (`#043626`).
- **Email Newsletter:** Borderless input embedded inside an ultra-light card container (`#eef2f6`), flanked by a deep green pill subscribe button.
